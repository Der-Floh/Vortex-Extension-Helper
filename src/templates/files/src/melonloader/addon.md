## MelonLoader

{{gameName}} mods need [MelonLoader](https://melonwiki.xyz). Vortex shows a notification with a link while it is missing. Install it with its own installer, or drop the `MelonLoader.x64.zip` from its [releases](https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases/latest) on Vortex's Mods page, enable it and deploy.
